Our story

A little kitchen with a big reason to celebrate.

Carvic began with one oven and orders arriving through DMs. The kitchen may be busier now, but the promise is the same: every bake starts with a real order and ends with a personal finishing touch.

We keep batches small, make each item fresh and customise the flavour, size and message to fit your moment.
